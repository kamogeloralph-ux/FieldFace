# FieldFace Client Policy

**Effective date:** 12 September 2026

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy explains how FieldFace records attendance, supports payroll administration, and handles information submitted by client companies and their employees. It applies to every company using FieldFace, every employee using the clocking application, and every administrator using a management or platform console.

FieldFace is an operational tool. It does not replace an employer’s legal duties, payroll review, employment policies, disciplinary processes, privacy notices, or record-retention obligations. Each client remains responsible for reviewing this policy with its legal, human-resources, payroll, and information-security advisers before using FieldFace with employees.

## 2. Information collected

FieldFace may process employee numbers, names, tax numbers, contact details, physical addresses, employee PIN credentials, hourly rates, site assignments, clock-in and clock-out times, GPS coordinates, GPS accuracy, geofence results, selfie photographs, shift hours, payslips, deductions, and administrator audit records.

The system stores a selfie and location evidence with each clocking action. A failed geofence check is recorded for management review; it is not silently discarded.

## 3. Why information is used

Information is used to authenticate employees, verify attendance at designated worksites, calculate hours, prepare management-issued payslips, apply approved payroll deductions, produce reports, support offline synchronization, protect the service from misuse, and maintain an audit trail of material administrative and payroll actions.

Tax numbers are retained for payroll and payslip details. Employees use employee numbers, not tax numbers, to access the clocking application.

## 4. Employee responsibilities

Employees must keep their PIN confidential, use their own employee number, permit the required camera and location permissions, clock in and out at the correct worksite, and report incorrect attendance, personal information, or payslip information promptly to their employer.

Employees must not use another person’s credentials, submit a manipulated image or location, or attempt to bypass a geofence or offline synchronization control.

## 5. Employer responsibilities

The client company must provide employees with an appropriate privacy notice and any required workplace or legal disclosures before collecting selfies, GPS information, tax information, or payroll data. The client must use a lawful and transparent basis for processing information and must not collect more information than is reasonably necessary for its stated purposes.

The client must keep employee and administrator records accurate, review flagged attendance records, approve payslips before relying on them for payroll, configure deductions fairly and accurately, restrict administrator access, and remove access when a person leaves the organization.

Company deductions may be configured for all employees or for selected employees. The client must document the reason for each deduction, confirm that the deduction is authorized, and verify that its amount and assignment comply with applicable employment and payroll requirements.

## 6. Attendance and offline operation

When a device is offline, FieldFace may temporarily store a queued clocking action on that device so that it can synchronize when connectivity returns. The action includes an identifier and capture time to reduce duplicate submissions. Employees should not repeatedly submit the same action while offline.

Offline operation does not guarantee that a clocking action will be accepted. The employer must review synchronization failures, device loss, incorrect device time, and disputed attendance records.

## 7. Payslips and deductions

Management users or platform administrators issue payslips. Employees cannot generate their own payslips. A generated payslip records the hours, rates, UIF deduction where configured, company deductions applied at generation time, gross pay, and net pay.

The deduction details on an issued payslip are a snapshot. Changing a company deduction later does not retroactively change an already generated payslip. Payroll administrators must review and finalize payroll periods only after checking hours, rates, deductions, and employee information.

FieldFace calculations are provided for operational assistance and must be reviewed by the responsible payroll administrator. The client remains responsible for statutory deductions, tax treatment, payment, and payslip compliance.

## 8. Access and security

Management users can administer only their company’s operational data. They cannot change locked company settings, employee rates, or employee activation status unless the platform administrator performs that action. Platform administrators can manage companies across the platform and must use that access only for authorized support and administration.

Users must protect passwords, PINs, persistent-login devices, and administrator sessions. Shared accounts are not permitted. Suspected unauthorized access, lost devices, exposed credentials, or incorrect access must be reported to the client administrator immediately.

## 9. Retention and deletion

The client determines retention periods subject to applicable employment, tax, payroll, privacy, and evidentiary obligations. Clocking photographs, GPS evidence, payslips, and audit records should be retained only for as long as a documented purpose or legal obligation requires. Deletion requests must be assessed against payroll, employment, dispute, and legal-retention requirements before action is taken.

Deleting a company or employee record may affect linked attendance, site, payslip, and audit data. Administrators must confirm that appropriate exports and backups exist before destructive actions.

## 10. Employee requests and complaints

Employees should first contact their employer’s designated administrator about access, correction, attendance, payroll, deduction, or deletion questions. The employer must investigate and respond according to its internal process and applicable law.

FieldFace support and privacy requests should be directed through the service operator’s designated support channel. Client companies must publish their own responsible contact details to employees.

## 11. Security incidents

A client that suspects unauthorized access, data loss, credential exposure, or a material privacy incident must preserve relevant evidence, disable affected access where appropriate, notify its responsible administrator, and follow its incident-response and legal-notification obligations. FieldFace deployment operators must investigate service-side incidents and communicate material impact through the agreed support channel.

## 12. Policy changes

This policy may be updated when FieldFace functionality, security controls, or legal requirements change. The effective date identifies the version currently presented in the application. Clients should keep a copy of the version provided to their employees.

> **Important:** This document is a product policy template, not legal advice. Each client must obtain independent legal, payroll, and privacy advice and adapt the policy before relying on it as an employment or privacy notice.
